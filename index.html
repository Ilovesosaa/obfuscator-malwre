Under the hood

A build pipeline that
delivers real results

Every stage runs server-side, in a fixed order, on a lexer that understands Lua and Luau. Turn any pass off and the artifact changes with your settings — no hidden stages, no surprises at load time.

One request, six passes

From tokenizing to chunk splitting, the whole rewrite happens while you wait. Each pass is toggleable and reported back with the build.
